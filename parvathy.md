# SIH26034 — Data Collection & Pitch Deck — Build Brief

## Project
Building compliance-checking software for the **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011** (Ministry of Consumer Affairs). It scans product label images and flags missing or illegal declarations.

## Team structure
6 members, 2 groups. You're in **Group 1**, led by the vision model owner, alongside another ECE member doing rules research. Group 2 (led by the 2nd CSE member) builds the rule-engine, UI/UX, and dashboard. Timeline: 3 sessions, Sep 1 / 2 / 3, 5–9pm each.

## Your role
Two connected jobs: give the vision model something real to be tested on, and turn the team's technical + regulatory work into the pitch.

### 1. Sample data collection
Collect **20–30 product label photos** spanning:
- Multiple categories: packaged food, cosmetics, electronics, household goods
- A deliberate mix of compliant and non-compliant labels (missing expiry date, missing manufacturer address, etc. — these become the demo's "gotcha" moments)
- Varying photo quality (angle, lighting) so the model is tested realistically, not just on perfect studio shots

### 2. Pitch deck
Structure to build toward:
- Problem framing (why mislabeled packaged goods are a real, current issue)
- Impact case (consumer harm, enforcement gap — this is where the rules researcher's findings on penalties/enforcement gaps feed in)
- Technical architecture (vision pipeline → rule-engine → report/dashboard)
- Demo script (walk through a real non-compliant label being caught live)

## Deliverables by session
- **Sep 1:** Collect the sample images; start the deck skeleton (section headers, rough narrative).
- **Sep 2:** Deck draft v1 with early architecture diagram and any screenshots available; finalize the sample image set for the vision model owner.
- **Sep 3:** Final deck polish, rehearse the demo script against the working system.

## Starting prompt for your AI
"Help me structure a hackathon pitch deck for a product that scans packaged-goods labels and detects Legal Metrology compliance violations. I need slides covering: problem, impact, technical architecture, and a live demo script. I'll also want help identifying realistic product-label photo sources for testing a compliance-detection model."
